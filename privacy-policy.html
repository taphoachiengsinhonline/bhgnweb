Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the BHGN Video Publisher application ("the App"), operated by Bách Hoá Giao Ngay ("we", "our", or "us"), collects, accesses, uses, stores, and protects information when interacting with third-party social media platforms, including but not limited to TikTok and Facebook (Meta).

1. Information We Access

The App connects to authorized social media accounts using official OAuth authentication and platform APIs solely for publishing content. Depending on the platform, we may access:

Data Purpose
OAuth access tokens Authenticate the App and authorize publishing content on behalf of the connected account.
Basic account or page information Verify account identity, available publishing permissions, and supported posting options.
Publishing status and post identifiers Track whether uploaded videos or posts were successfully published.
Available account settings required for publishing Determine supported privacy settings or publishing capabilities provided by the platform.

The App only accesses information necessary to publish content. We do not access private conversations, personal messages, friends lists, followers lists, user analytics beyond what is required for publishing, or data belonging to unrelated users.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect personal information from followers, viewers, or other users of connected social media accounts.
  • We do not use social platform data for advertising, profiling, or selling user information.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share access tokens or platform account data with third parties.
  • We do not access information that is unrelated to publishing content.

3. How We Use the Information

Information obtained from connected platforms is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate authorized accounts.
  • Publish promotional videos, images, or posts.
  • Monitor publishing status.
  • Maintain internal publishing records.
  • Troubleshoot publishing failures.

The App does not publish content without authorization from the connected account owner.

4. Data Storage and Retention

OAuth access tokens and related publishing credentials are stored securely on our own server infrastructure using industry-standard security practices. These credentials are retained only while the account remains connected. Users may revoke access at any time through the corresponding platform settings, or request deletion by contacting us.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect stored credentials against unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. Access is restricted to systems required for content publishing.

6. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with official APIs provided by third-party platforms, including TikTok and Meta (Facebook), as well as AI-powered content generation services. Each third-party service operates under its own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. Only the information necessary to perform publishing operations is exchanged with those services.

7. User Rights

Users may:

  • Disconnect their social media account at any time.
  • Revoke API permissions through their TikTok or Facebook account settings.
  • Request deletion of stored credentials.
  • Contact us regarding any privacy-related questions.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any revisions will be posted on this page together with the updated revision date.
