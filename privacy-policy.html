Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the "BHGN Video Publisher" application ("the App"), operated in connection with Bách Hoá Giao Ngay ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, and protects information when interacting with TikTok's APIs.

1. Information We Access

The App integrates with TikTok using the Content Posting API. Through this integration, we access and process the following information, limited strictly to what is required to publish video content:

DataPurpose
TikTok account access token (OAuth)Authenticate the App to publish videos on behalf of the authorized account only.
Basic creator info (via Query Creator Info endpoint)Confirm posting permissions and target account settings before publishing (e.g. allowed privacy levels, duet/stitch settings).
Publish status data (post ID, processing status)Confirm whether a video was successfully published, and to update our internal records.

We do not access private messages, follower lists, analytics data, or any content belonging to other TikTok users.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect personal information from TikTok viewers or followers of the connected account.
  • We do not use TikTok data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to publishing our own product videos.
  • We do not sell or share TikTok data with third parties.

3. How We Use the Information

The information described above is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate the App's connection to our own authorized TikTok business account.
  • Publish AI-generated promotional videos to that account via the Content Posting API.
  • Confirm publishing success or failure for internal record-keeping.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Access tokens are stored securely on our own server infrastructure and are not shared with any third party. Tokens are retained only for as long as the connected account remains active with the App, and are deleted upon disconnection or upon request.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect stored credentials, including restricting access to authorized personnel and systems only.

6. Third-Party Services

The App relies on TikTok's official APIs and an AI video-generation service to create video content. These third-party services operate under their own privacy policies, and no TikTok user data is shared with the video-generation service beyond the final rendered video file itself.

7. Your Rights

If you are the owner of a TikTok account connected to this App, you may revoke access at any time via your TikTok account settings, or by contacting us to request deletion of any stored credentials.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
